# Iceland — recording and photo brief

Status: language-toolkit script prepared; assets missing. Use `media.yaml` as the asset
inventory. This brief covers pronunciation and place media only; it does not establish a
complete research-backed Iceland briefing or require users to enter practice mode.

## Recording direction

Book one native Icelandic main voice and a second native Icelandic voice for the local
scenario lines. The reviewer approves text, pronunciation, register and meanings before
recording. Do not lift dictionary/course recordings: a reference is not a redistribution license.

Record every row below twice: normal conversational pace, then a naturally re-spoken slow
take. Do not time-stretch. The slow take must remain understandable and natural. Compare
every respelling against the delivered audio; rewrite it when it encourages the wrong sound.

For phrase stems, record only the spoken words. Do not read an ellipsis or invent a name.
The Alex example is display-only; the app must not pronounce a user's personal name from a
stock recording. Directions and short responses must fit the situation, rather than sound
like reading a dictionary.

Delivery: mono Ogg Vorbis, 44.1 kHz, approximately 64 kb/s; −16 LUFS integrated, true peak
≤ −1 dBTP; 150 ms silence at each edge. Each clip must be ≤ 6 s, including the slow take.
If natural approved wording cannot meet that limit, resolve the wording or specification
before recording; do not speed up a speaker to pass.

Use media.yaml for the exact spoken-item inventory; each item requires two takes.
Some words repeat intentionally under different stable item IDs; the future compiler may
deduplicate identical approved audio bytes without changing IDs.

## Photo assignments

Use authentic photos with owned, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA rights. A tourism site's image credit
does not grant reuse permission. Keep exact source, author, license version, permission where
applicable, acquisition date and SHA-256 in CREDITS.csv. No invented credits or stock placeholders.

| Sight / file stem | Assignment |
| --- | --- |
| sight-laugardalslaug | Pool exterior or authorized pool-area photo; no changing rooms or identifiable bathers without permission. |
| sight-thingvellir | Wide rift-valley view with the marked path; show the human scale without people posing on edges. |
| sight-gullfoss | Both drops from an authorized viewpoint; no drone or cliff-edge pose. |
| sight-geysir | Strokkur eruption with safe viewing boundary visible; do not label it as Great Geysir. |
| sight-hallgrimskirkja | Exterior showing the stepped facade from a public viewpoint; no recognizable worshippers. |
| sight-harpa | Glass facade and harbor context; choose a photo with redistribution rights, not a press image. |
| sight-national-museum | Museum exterior or specifically licensed exhibit image; admission does not grant reproduction rights. |
| sight-seljalandsfoss | Front-facing waterfall view; do not imply the rear path is always open. |
| sight-skogafoss | Full waterfall from the designated base area; keep the water's force visible. |
| sight-reynisfjara | Coastline from a safe permitted viewpoint; no people at the waterline or under unstable cliffs. |
| sight-jokulsarlon | Icebergs from shore; no staged climbing on ice. Photograph lagoon and label accurately. |
| sight-godafoss | Broad curved falls from a designated viewpoint; do not advertise an informal river-edge route. |
| sight-myvatn | Lake, lava and bird habitat; avoid substituting a nearby commercial bathing lagoon. |

Deliver original JPEG sources; the future compiler produces 1280×720 WebP ≤ 250 KB and a
480×270 thumbnail. Check framing in both sizes. No recognizable people without appropriate
permission. Do not portray unsafe behavior as an aspirational travel shot.

## Sign-off

The native reviewer listens to every take and checks spelling and respelling together.
A separate safety/editorial pass checks emergency and allergy wording, source support,
on-site map entrances and access notes. A US reader attempts the five arrival tasks using
the actual recordings and cards. Record reviewer role, review date, pack version and exact
approved file hashes; do not replace a pending status with a generic reviewed label.
