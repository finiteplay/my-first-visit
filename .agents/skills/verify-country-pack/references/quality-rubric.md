# Country-content quality rubric

Score each core briefing or playbook from 0 to 2 for every criterion:

- `0` — absent, misleading or unusable;
- `1` — present but thin, generic, weakly supported or difficult to use;
- `2` — specific, supported, audience-appropriate and decision-useful.

## Critical criteria

Any zero is a failing gate.

| Criterion | Passing evidence |
|---|---|
| User decision | Names the decision, experience or surprise the section helps with |
| Evidence fit | Material claims use the correct source roles and valid dates |
| Anecdote integrity | Context, event, action and outcome are preserved; no anecdote becomes a statistic |
| Contrasting experience | Credible disagreement/counterexample is represented, or the absence is stated |
| Sensitive-topic control | Safety, health, legal, emergency and insurance claims have required verification and live handoffs |
| Actionability | Gives preparation steps, options, trade-offs and a next action |
| Uncertainty | Says what varies, why it may vary and what the user must verify or decide |
| Audience lens | Advice fits the declared traveler origin and trip profile without pretending it is universal |
| Travel-mode fit | Materially different destination modes were discovered, separated and given mode-specific trade-offs |
| Season fit | Month rankings name the traveler goal and trade-offs; clothing changes concretely by season without presenting climate as a forecast |
| Originality and rights | Original prose; no copied source structure or unlicensed media |

## Experience criteria

Every core section must total at least 10 of 12, with no zero.

| Criterion | Passing evidence |
|---|---|
| Hook | Opens with a concrete surprise, desire, scene or consequential choice |
| Pacing | Leads with the useful answer; details are progressively disclosed |
| Human texture | Uses specific, well-contextualized experience rather than generic encyclopedia prose |
| Editorial value | Offers a labeled, defensible take instead of dumping links or hiding behind neutrality |
| Positive choice | Helps choose an experience to repeat as well as avoiding problems |
| Language connection | Connects phrases/signs only where they help the experience; no forced quiz interruption |

## Whole-pack checks

- The travel-mode inventory is evidence-backed rather than inherited from a generic template.
- At least one useful path exists for each declared primary trip mode; meaningful exclusions
  are explained, and each primary mode receives a traveler-task test.
- Important topics are not dominated by one platform or a single viral story.
- The pack contains ordinary and successful experiences, not only failures.
- Fast-changing facts are visibly live/provider-specific rather than frozen offline.
- Optional practice is reachable but does not gate, interrupt or dominate the briefing.
- Every unfinished review, missing perspective and weak signal remains visible.

## Traveler-task report

For each task record:

```text
Task:
Traveler profile:
Content provided:
Time to usable answer:
Options identified:
Trade-offs identified:
Preparation steps identified:
Uncertainty/live check identified:
Next action identified:
Confusion or unsupported inference:
PASS/FAIL:
Revision and rerun:
```

Pass when the reviewer finds all five decision elements—options, trade-offs, preparation,
uncertainty and next action—within 90 seconds without inventing a material fact.
